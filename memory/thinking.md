# Thinking checklist (read before every reply)

Use this before writing any draft or answer. Do not skip steps.

1. Read rules.md (top section) and memory/owner-preferences.md.
2. Timeline. Write down, with times from the screenshot:
   - Customer's first message.
   - Our (owner's) first reply, including voice notes. A voice note counts as a greeting.
   - Customer's latest message.
   - Check the earliest and latest times, not just the newest message.
3. Greeting check. If we already greeted (voice note, earlier text), do not greet again and do not write "thank you for reaching out". Start directly.
4. Meaning of the screenshot. What does the customer want: price, lily types, date, budget, shipping time? Which ad did they reply to?
5. What has happened: already answered, already greeted, price given, budget/size/date known, record in cloud.md.
6. What has not happened: missing budget, size, date, area, shipping time, owner's decision.
7. Price rule. Do not quote a single item price first. Give a range only if the customer asks, and only from recorded prices. Ask budget, size or volume, and exact date.
8. Budget rule. Heart bouquet 47,500. Budget around 40,000 or 35,000 or lower: round bouquet, not heart. Budget far below our range (about 6,000 now): owner decides.
9. Language rule. Replies to owner: Roman Urdu or Roman English only. No Hindi words, no Devanagari.
10. Draft for customer: copy box, English if customer writes English. Owner approves before sending. I cannot send messages.
11. After the reply: update cloud.md, add a changelog line, commit and push, and tell the owner which file changed.
